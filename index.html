Pattern Design

Patterns that build visual identity.

Pattern design creates repeatable visual elements that can make a brand, product or social campaign feel more consistent. Useful for backgrounds, packaging, stationery and digital content.

Tute Cover Design

Covers that make content easier to notice.

A strong tute cover helps students identify a subject quickly while making educational material look organised and professional. Useful for tuition notes, handouts and digital PDFs.

Video Editing

Edits built for attention.

Good editing controls pacing, mood and information so viewers stay engaged. Useful for reels, promotions, event highlights, social media and short-form storytelling.

About Me

Creative work with a personal touch.

I’m 22 years old and work as a freelance Graphic Designer & Video Editor. I have around 3 years of experience in graphic design, building my skills through real creative work and continuous practice.

My main design tools are Adobe Illustrator and Adobe Photoshop. I also use Canva when a project needs flexible templates or quick content, and CapCut for video editing. Alongside freelance work, I run OBLIVIA GRAPHICS, where I create and share design work.

I like taking an idea that starts rough and turning it into something clear, modern and useful — whether that is a visual identity, a cover, a social post or a short video.
